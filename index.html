OUR ORIGINS

Bridging the Gap for Ambitious Students.

Ever since the pandemic started, we noticed a shift. Students felt rushed, especially in High School. We saw ambitious students already stressed about getting ready to 'leave the nest.'

We realized that the transition to higher education is difficult enough without doing it alone. 'Road to A' was born to provide that necessary aid—bridging the gap between high school pressure and university success.
